yoink

Send files straight to them.

Peer-to-peer transfer from your terminal or your browser. No accounts, no uploads, nothing stored in between.

Open web app Get the CLI 
# sender
$ yoink send photos/ notes.txt --qr
# receiver, anywhere
$ yoink get <ticket>
o-o-o-o-o-. . . . .  52%  4s left
  • DirectFiles go from device to device, encrypted. Nobody in the middle keeps a copy.
  • One short ticketShare a line of text or a QR code. Scan it with a phone and the web app opens ready to receive.
  • Folders and bundlesSend several files and folders at once. They travel as one compressed bundle and unpack on arrival.
  • CLI and browser speak the same languageSend from the terminal, receive in a tab. Or the other way around.

Install the CLI

Cargo
cargo install --git https://github.com/jetdafih/yoink yoink
Prebuilt
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jetdafih/yoink/cli/install.sh | sh

Browser transfers go through a relay, so they are slower than CLI to CLI. Files up to 512 MB work in any browser; larger downloads stream straight to disk in Chrome and Edge. For anything huge, use the CLI.
